GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
NATIONAL HEALTH AUTHORITY (NHA) & MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE

AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB-PMJAY)

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government-funded healthcare assurance scheme, launched in September 2018. It aims to provide universal health coverage and financial protection to over 12 crore vulnerable rural and urban families (approx 55 crore beneficiaries), representing the bottom 40% of the Indian population. In 2024, the scheme expanded to cover all senior citizens aged 70 years and above irrespective of their income status.

2. HEALTH COVERAGE AND BENEFIT PACKAGE
- Health Cover: ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across all empaneled public and private hospitals nationwide.
- Cashless and Paperless Access: 100% cashless treatment at the point of care.
- Pre and Post-Hospitalization: Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization diagnostic and medication expenses.
- Over 1,949 medical and surgical procedures covered, including oncology, cardiology, neurosurgery, and intensive care treatments.
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender. Pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one of enrollment.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Beneficiaries identified automatically based on deprivation and occupational criteria in the Socio-Economic and Caste Census (SECC 2011) database and National Food Security Act (NFSA) ration cards.
- All senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for dedicated Top-Up/Ayushman cards regardless of economic category.

4. AYUSHMAN CARD CREATION & REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for identity verification).
- Ration Card / Family ID document.
- Mobile Number for e-KYC.
Citizens can check eligibility and download their Ayushman Card (Golden Card) through the Ayushman App or the official portal at https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in.

5. IMPLEMENTING AGENCY AND CONTACT HELPLINE
- Central Nodal Body: National Health Authority (NHA), Government of India.
- State Implementing Body: State Health Agencies (SHA).
- National Toll-Free Helpline: 14555.
- Official Website: https://nha.gov.in / https://pmjay.gov.in.
