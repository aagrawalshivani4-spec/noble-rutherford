DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE

PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN) GUIDELINES

1. SCHEME OVERVIEW AND PURPOSE
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Hon'ble Prime Minister on 24th February 2019 to augment the income of all landholding farmer families across the country. The scheme aims to assist small, marginal, and all eligible farming households in meeting their financial needs for procuring various agricultural inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs.

2. FINANCIAL BENEFIT AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
Under the PM-KISAN scheme, financial assistance of ₹6,000 per annum is provided to all eligible landholder farmer families. The amount is directly credited to the Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts of beneficiaries in three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each:
- Period 1: April to July (₹2,000)
- Period 2: August to November (₹2,000)
- Period 3: December to March (₹2,000)
All payments are transferred 100% via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS).

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- All landholding farmer families having cultivable landholding in their names are eligible.
- Mandatory e-KYC compliance and Aadhaar seeding with the bank account.
- Land record details must be verified and updated on the state land revenue registry portal.

4. EXCLUSIONS FROM BENEFITS
The following categories of higher economic status are excluded:
- Institutional landholders.
- Former and present holders of constitutional posts, Ministers, MPs, MLAs, MLCs, Mayors, and District Panchayat Chairpersons.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and autonomous institutions (excluding Class IV/Multi-Tasking Staff).
- All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

5. REGISTRATION AND REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Farmers can register self-online via the PM-KISAN portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) or mobile app, or through Common Service Centres (CSCs) and local Village Revenue Officers.
Required Documents:
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory).
- Land ownership document (Khasra/Khatauni / RTC / RoR).
- Active Bank Account Passbook (IFSC & Account Number).
- Valid Mobile Number linked with Aadhaar for OTP verification.
